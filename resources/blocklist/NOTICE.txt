Cra blocklist: third-party notices
==================================

domains.txt is the content-blocking list behind Settings > Privacy > "Block
third-party ads and trackers". It is the union of the two lists below, reduced
to bare domains (hosts-file syntax, comments, localhost entries and invalid
names removed) and with entries dropped when a parent domain is already listed,
because Cra blocks a listed domain together with its subdomains. Fetched
2026-10-25.

1. AdAway default blocklist
   Source:  https://github.com/AdAway/adaway.github.io/blob/master/hosts.txt
